**Contract rolls this week:** none (WTI and Brent front contracts unchanged).

## What changed and why

1. WTI jumped $9.56, or 10.4%, to $101.41 and Brent rose $5.14, or 4.9%, to $109.86, its highest settlement since May 19, which narrowed the Brent-WTI spread from $12.87 to $8.45.
2. Reports tied the rally to supply worries around the Persian Gulf and uncertainty over Russian diesel availability, along with attacks on Russian oil infrastructure.
3. WTI outperformed Brent in part because it was recovering from the hurricane disruption and the previous week's large US crude draw, which is the explanation EnergyNow gives.
4. Both contracts are the same as the week before, November WTI and December Brent, so the weekly changes are like-for-like; November WTI expires Oct 20 and December Brent on Oct 30, so each of the next two weeks will carry a roll flag.
5. I'm watching whether crude stocks keep falling after the 8.6 mb draw and whether the pullback that followed the US easing some Russian energy restrictions holds.
